# Enemy and Magis integration

Player input and enemy utility decisions converge on the same SpellCastingComponent. EnemyCombat owns an explicit EnemyAbilityData loadout and checks eligibility before submitting. Enemy.can_cast_spell authorizes only referenced spell resources. The casting component revalidates authority, statuses, resources, targets, mana and cooldowns. It handles preparation/channeling/costs and dispatches through the same SpellDelivery and SpellExecutor as the player.

Ordinary attacks submit CombatRequest effects using SpellCastContext with a null spell. Source, direction, modifiers, effect budget, depth and authority remain meaningful. DamageEffect supports this context and uses an ordinary hit colour when no spell exists. No fake SpellData is needed for melee. Hitbox.receive_damage adds source attribution; the existing _apply_damage and receive_hit APIs remain callable. Enemy threat uses the source signal; old receive_hit callers without source information cannot identify a separate attacker and do not apply the defender's outgoing damage modifier. Outgoing damage scaling is separate from healing/echo magnitude scaling.

Damage, healing, statuses, knockback and stats keep their existing resolution paths. Stat configuration sets incoming/outgoing multipliers, defense and duration-based status resistance. Typed ApplyStatusEffect and legacy hitbox status helpers both use the resistance gate. ActiveEffectManager retains independent bounded stacking, owner ticks and expiry. Enemy regen/speed/cast-speed/damage/defense buffs are applied through the relevant shared/actor components; player-only dash mechanics are not granted to enemies.

Projectile dispatch initializes movement/signals after parenting, selects collision masks from faction, captures world coordinates and calls start() exactly once. Swept movement and existing body impacts remain. Reflection creates an independent context and now transfers lifecycle ownership as well as team/heading/hit history. Additional factions can query both actor collision layers and reject allies through CombatFactions. Relationship rules also apply to beams, projectile fields, constructs and decasts.

CombatLifecycle marks spawned spell nodes and entities with their owner. Enemy and boss death/despawn cleans their owned projectiles, persistent areas, constructs and minions. Reflected projectiles belong to the reflecting caster. Applied statuses on other actors stay target-owned until expiry, preserving the existing spell-pipeline policy.

Boss phase logic remains an extension. Melee/dash/AOE use CombatRequest; bursts use shared casting and preserve their configured interval. Boss magical bursts use the actual spell payload; per-shot costs are ceiling-divided from the configured spell upfront cost. Existing phase attack selection, transition invincibility, minion scenes and special motion remain separate from ordinary utility AI.

All gameplay execution is server-only with a multiplayer peer. No client request RPC is added. The tests use ENet peers to prove unauthorized local enemy simulation and damage are blocked; they do not validate full network replication. Multiplayer enemy snapshots, client visual/cast events, synchronized projectiles, late join and per-player rewards remain future work.
