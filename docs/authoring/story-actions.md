# Story actions

Add StoryAction Resources to stage `on_exit_actions`, transition `actions`, or stage `on_enter_actions`. `SetStoryFlagAction` validates a registered typed flag and delegates campaign booleans to QuestManager; campaign writes remain optional because external flag mutation is not atomic with story transitions. `SetStoryVariableAction` sets, increments or decrements transient or registered persistent typed variables. `SetStoryObjectiveAction` writes the runtime main-story hint without touching quest goal lines. `EmitStorySignalAction` emits a typed story notification. `RequestStoryAutosaveAction` reports deferred and cannot be required because the asynchronous save may fail. `SetStoryChoiceAction` refuses implicit execution; use the explicit confirmed-choice API. Required actions must return SUCCESS or NO_CHANGE before the stage advances. Avoid mixing irreversible effects with required actions until durable transactions exist.
